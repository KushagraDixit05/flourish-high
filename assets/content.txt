#About FHI
Flourish High International is a global trading and sourcing company dedicated to connecting India’s diverse ecosystem of manufacturers, suppliers, and artisans with international markets. We work closely with a trusted network of partners across textiles, handicrafts, and agro products, enabling global buyers to access high-quality, competitively sourced products. Our approach combines deep local sourcing expertise with a strong understanding of international market expectations. At Flourish High International, we focus on building transparent, reliable, and long-term trade relationships.

#Building Reliable Trade Bridges Between India and Global Markets
Flourish High International works across handicrafts, home décor, textiles, agricultural products, and emerging sourcing categories through trusted supplier networks aligned with global buyer requirements.

#Our Trade Focus
Connecting global markets with India's manufacturing excellence

Textiles & Hospitality Linen
We supply standardized export-quality towels, bed linen, pillow covers, floor mats, and kitchen textiles for hospitality, retail, and institutional buyers across global markets. Indian Handicrafts & Home Décor
We connect global buyers with curated handcrafted and functional décor products sourced from India's artisan clusters. Our focus combines traditional craftsmanship with contemporary designs suited for modern retail and lifestyle markets.

Custom Sourcing for Global Buyers
Through our supplier network across India, we support tailored sourcing requirements including spices, paper and stationery products, carpets and rugs, select machinery, and other emerging trade categories based on buyer needs.

#Frequently Asked Questions
Everything you need to know about working with FHI


What products does FHI trade?
Flourish High International supports global buyers across multiple export-ready product categories from India. Our core focus includes textiles such as towels, bed linen, pillow covers and floor mats, along with Indian handicrafts and contemporary home décor products. We also supply carpets and rugs, paper and stationery products, and selected spices through verified supplier networks. In addition, we support custom sourcing requirements based on buyer specifications across emerging product categories.

Do you manufacture products?
No, FHI is a trading and sourcing company. We connect global buyers with trusted Indian manufacturers and artisans who produce the products.

Can you help source custom products?
Yes, absolutely. We work closely with buyers to understand their specific requirements and connect them with suppliers who can fulfill custom orders tailored to their markets.

Do you support export logistics?
Yes, we provide complete export support including quality checks, packaging, documentation, and shipping coordination to ensure smooth international delivery.

Who can contact FHI?
We welcome enquiries from importers, distributors, sourcing partners, retailers, and any business interested in Indian products or establishing trade relationships.
